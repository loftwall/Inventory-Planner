MATERIAL DETAIL

How this preview calculates demand

Near-term demand includes uninspected Pending Fulfillment orders dated from the report date through the cutoff, inclusive. Overdue lines are included when the checkbox is on. Missing dates are flagged, included in demand, and treated as today.

Material required = unfulfilled item quantity converted to parent units, with whole pieces packed by Parent Yield, sharing leftovers within an order and parent.

Planning available = usable on hand − near-term material demand. Future demand is shown separately.

Projected balance = planning available + remaining quantity from all approved open POs. If this is below the reorder point, the suggested order restores the reorder point, rounded up to whole units.

Timing risk is flagged if stock goes below zero before incoming supply arrives. Same-day arrivals count as available for shipping demand. Missing and overdue PO arrival dates need review.

Units are tracked per material. Suggested orders are shown as quantities in pieces; no prices or costs are shown. No NetSuite reservations are changed.
